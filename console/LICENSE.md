## What you may do without paying

Install the Console on a server of yours and use it, for work too, within the free threshold the Console itself
shows in its "Licence" card. Today that is up to 3 PCs of a single customer.

## Beyond the free threshold

To follow more PCs or more customers you need a licence file, issued by the author for your installation. It is
valid for the installation, the numbers and the period written in it. You request it from the "Licence" card of
the Console.

The licence is a subscription: there is no lifetime licence. Prices are those published on <https://omissis.eu> on
the day of the order.

- **Companies, firms and public bodies:** the licence lasts 12 months and is paid in advance. On request the
  commitment can be 24 or 36 months, at a locked price and invoiced yearly. The number of PCs can be raised at any
  time by paying the difference for the months left; it is lowered at renewal.
- **Those who follow several customers (MSPs, resellers):** the licence renews month by month and is paid at the end
  of the month on the PCs followed in that month, with no minimum term.
- **At expiry** the Console warns in good time, keeps working for the grace period shown in its "Licence" card and
  then goes back to the free threshold. The data collected stays yours.

The Console runs on your server and sends nothing to the author. What it stores (counts, program names and,
unless you turn them off, the names of computers and users) is in your hands: with the names, the Console shows
how much each person uses AI services, and in many countries that is subject to the rules on monitoring employees.
